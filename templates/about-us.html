Our Story

About Spendly

We believe that understanding where your money goes is the first step toward financial freedom. Spendly was built to make that simple.

Our Mission

Money is personal — but most financial tools feel cold and complicated. Spendly exists to change that. Our mission is to give every person a clear, honest picture of their spending so they can make informed decisions without the noise.

We keep things simple: log an expense, see where it fits, understand the pattern. No subscriptions to link, no algorithms guessing your habits — just you and your numbers.

What We Build

Spendly is a personal expense tracker designed for everyday use. Here is what you can do with it:

  • Log expenses by category, amount, date, and description
  • View monthly and custom-date-range summaries
  • Understand your spending patterns through category breakdowns
  • Edit or delete any entry — your data, your control

We build every feature with one question in mind: does this make it easier to understand your finances? If the answer is no, we leave it out.

Who We Are

Spendly is an independent project started by a small team of developers who were tired of overpaying for tools that did too much and explained too little. We are based in India and built Spendly with Indian users in mind — rupee-first, mobile-friendly, and offline-capable.

We are a tiny team. That means every decision is deliberate, every feature earns its place, and feedback from users actually changes what we build next.
